**Scarlet Meter 0.2.0**, a DPS meter for AION 2 with its own overlay on top of
[RATmeter](https://github.com/Kuroukihime/AIon2-Dps-Meter)'s packet decoding (RATmeter v1.11.5.341, as in 0.1.0).

> **Early release.** Its in-game checks are not finished, so numbers may be off. Reports are welcome in
> [Issues](https://github.com/harrynguon/Scarlet-Meter/issues).

## Download and run

1. Install [Npcap](https://npcap.com) with **"Install Npcap in WinPcap API-compatible Mode"** ticked (and
   "Restrict Npcap driver's access to Administrators only" unticked, so the meter needs no administrator rights).
2. Download `ScarletMeter-0.2.0-win-x64.zip` below and unzip it anywhere you can write to (Documents, Desktop; not
   Program Files). Updating from 0.1.0: unzip it over a copy elsewhere, then move your old `data` folder (settings,
   history, logs) into the new `ScarletMeter` folder.
3. Start the game, then run `ScarletMeter\ScarletMeter.exe`. Nothing else to install: .NET is built in.

Settings, history and logs stay in a `data` folder beside the exe. To see the meter with made-up fights, open a
terminal in the `ScarletMeter` folder and run `.\ScarletMeter.exe --demo`. Windows may warn that the file is from an
unknown publisher (it is not code-signed): choose "More info", then "Run anyway". The `.sha256` files have each
zip's checksum (`Get-FileHash` in PowerShell).

## What's new

- **An icon and a face.** The meter has its own icon on the exe and every window, and the overlay's header starts
  with Scarlet's face. Its ring follows the boss's HP: green above 66%, yellow from 33%, red below, its glow going out
  when nothing has hit the boss for a while, grey once it is down.
- **Readable rows.** Names, numbers and death counts sit on a crisp dark edge, names are bold, the death count is
  white-ish, the class icons are a little larger, and each row reads DPS, then the share over the total damage.
  Your own row is ringed instead of striped.
- **World events out of the way.** The notices fold into one slim bar under the rows (click it for the list; it stays
  as you left it), never cover a bar, use a calmer gold and step aside during boss fights. Nothing in the overlay
  scrolls: the meter grows to fit and returns to your size.
- **Boss timers.** The hourglass in the header opens [aion2timers.com](https://www.aion2timers.com/world-boss-timer/)'s
  world-boss timers in a small window of their own, which remembers your server and faction.
- **Fixes.** The background picture fits the panel again, whatever the window's height; scrolling a table in the
  details or history window no longer shows rows above its header; Spiritmaster is named as in the game; a crown
  marks a boss.
